## Notes / what this proves

- Two boundary conditions (settlement-window close, ring-caught fraction) are
  each tested on both sides of the line, not just "comfortably inside" — these
  are exactly the kind of off-by-one spots that silently change results.
- `false_positives` logic distinguishes "flagged + is actually fraud" (correct,
  not a false positive) from "flagged + is NOT fraud" (false positive) — these
  are easy to conflate and the two dedicated tests exist specifically to keep
  them apart.
