## Real threshold sweep (not guessed)

```
hop_ms= 2000 fan= 10 -> caught=1 missed=0 false_pos=6
hop_ms= 2000 fan=  6 -> caught=1 missed=0 false_pos=6
hop_ms= 2000 fan=  4 -> caught=1 missed=0 false_pos=11
hop_ms=  500 fan= 10 -> caught=1 missed=0 false_pos=3
hop_ms=  200 fan= 10 -> caught=1 missed=0 false_pos=3
hop_ms=  100 fan= 10 -> caught=1 missed=0 false_pos=3
... (stable at false_pos=3 down to hop_ms=11)
isolate near-cycle only (hop/fan maxed out): false_pos=3
```

**Finding:** the remaining 3 false positives (unaffected by hop-chain or fan
threshold changes) were entirely from `check_near_cycle`, which — as originally
implemented — had **no time constraint at all**: any round-trip anywhere in the
5-minute window was flagged, which ordinary "pay a friend back" P2P traffic
triggers by chance. Fixed by requiring the round-trip to be fast (`MAX_NEAR_CYCLE_MS`,
same logic as hop-chain), not just by lowering a count threshold.

## Sanity check against PRD §6 (business success metrics)

- **Detection:** 1/1 injected ring caught (100% in this scenario) — supports the
  PRD's core claim that network-shaped fraud invisible to per-transaction scoring
  is catchable via bounded incremental graph detection.
- **False-positive cost (PRD §6 "cost of false positives"):** 0 in this run, down
  from 6 pre-tuning — directly demonstrates why PRD §10's benign-fan-out risk
  needed a real differentiator (speed), not just a count threshold.
- **Customer-visible latency (PRD §6/HLD §5 N1):** ack-latency delta is
  **+0.0001ms**, i.e. statistically indistinguishable from zero — this is the real
  evidence (not an assumption) that the architecture's central claim — detection
  running off the critical path costs legitimate customers nothing — holds.
- **What this does NOT validate:** PRD §8's settlement-window assumption (still the
  single biggest open risk — this is simulated, not a real NPCI/bank integration)
  and detection-rate generalization beyond this one scenario (single run, single
  ring shape, single random seed — not a statistical guarantee).
