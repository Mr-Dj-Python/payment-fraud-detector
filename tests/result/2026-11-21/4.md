## A real design gap found and resolved during implementation

LLD §3.3 specified `AccountStats.counterparty_counts` as a single `Counter` across
both edge directions. Fan-out/fan-in detection needs **direction-separated**
distinct-counterparty counts, which that single counter can't answer on its own.
Rather than reopening the already-tested `graph_store.py`/`models.py`, the detector
recomputes direction-filtered counts directly from the (window-bounded) `edges`
deque — still local/bounded per ADR-001, just O(window size) instead of O(1) for
this one check. Documented in a `ponytail:` comment in `detector.py`.

## Notes / what this proves

- The benign-fan-out false positive is the single biggest open risk named in
  **PRD §10** — this test run is the first real evidence that Phase 0's heuristics
  reproduce it, not just a theoretical concern. It's expected to show up again,
  quantified, in Chunk 8's end-to-end false-positive-rate metric.
- `detect()` is exercised against a full multi-hop ring built through the real
  `graph_store.insert_transaction` path, not a hand-built fixture — this is the
  first test that exercises the simulator → graph store → detector pipeline together.
